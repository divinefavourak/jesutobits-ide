**An AI agent that builds in front of you**, now as a desktop app built on VS Code, for Windows, Linux and macOS.

Describe what you want in plain words; Jesutobits plans it, edits your files, runs your project and shows it to you live, asking before risky steps.

## What's new in 0.3.0

- **Built on VS Code.** The editor, terminal, Git and extensions (from Open VSX) are VS Code's; Jesutobits adds the agent, the live preview, visual editing and the Studio look.
- **Get started in a minute.** A welcome popup: where you are starting from, one of six themes, connecting OpenRouter, how much the agent checks with you, and your first idea.
- **Sign in with OpenRouter.** No key to copy. Or start with a **free coding model** (Laguna S 2.1 by Poolside); Jesutobits tells you its maker may learn from your prompts and asks first.
- **Your account.** Your picture or initial in the top-right corner: your profile, your OpenRouter plan, spend and free requests left today, your model and privacy choice.
- **A preview for every kind of project.** Visual editing for React + Vite; dev servers for anything with a `dev` or `start` script; plain HTML/CSS/JS sites and canvas games with live reload; any other start command (Python, Go, Rust...), with live output for programs that have no web page.
- **The agent runs commands and your app**, with your approval: install, test, type-check, build, preview.
- **A companion** that shows what the agent is really doing.

The full list is in the [changelog](https://github.com/divinefavourak/jesutobits/blob/v0.3.0/CHANGELOG.md).

## The first open

These builds are not signed with paid certificates yet, so each system may warn once:

- **Windows:** "Windows protected your PC": choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **macOS:** after the first try, open **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway** for Jesutobits.
- **Linux:** no warning. Saved keys use your desktop's keyring (GNOME Keyring or KWallet).

## Good to know

- This is an early release. Windows is tested by hand, Linux under WSL; **the macOS builds have not been opened on a real Mac yet**. Reports are welcome in [Issues](https://github.com/divinefavourak/jesutobits-ide/issues).
- You need an [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai) account for the agent (free to create; the free model costs nothing).
- There are no automatic updates yet: download the next release to update.
- Based on VS Code 1.143.1 (Code-OSS, MIT). "Visual Studio Code" is a Microsoft trademark and is not used here.
